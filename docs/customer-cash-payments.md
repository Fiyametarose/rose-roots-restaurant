# Customer cash payment and receipts

Open My Orders, then View My Bill, then Payment and receipt on an authenticated Customer terminal. The dialog retrieves the visit balance and bill version over HTTPS. Cash is selectable only when the backend advertises it and the visit has a payable, unblocked balance. Selecting Cash and requesting payment confirms the displayed amount and version. Staff must confirm collection through the Manager workflow.

The client durably records the UUID idempotency key and reviewed bill version in per-user Java Preferences before sending a cash request. Entries are scoped by backend origin and server-issued session. The journal stores identifiers only. On reconnection the client reads the recorded attempt or the balance's open attempt ID. If the request outcome remains unknown and no attempt ID can be read, the explicit Refresh / Recover action replays the same payload and key. Polling alone never starts a payment. Cancelling requests staff resolution; it does not end a pending payment locally. A failed or cancelled attempt can be replaced after the customer reviews the current balance again.

Receipts are fetched only after the backend reports SUCCEEDED and supplies a receipt ID. The client verifies the receipt's session, attempt ID and amount before displaying it. The existing My Orders and Reviews workflows remain separate.

The published balance exposes a pending attempt ID but no settled attempt or receipt locator. A terminal with its original journal can read the verified receipt while it retains the visit context, including server closure. Recovery on a replacement or reimaged terminal, or after the app can no longer reopen a closed visit, needs a shared, authorized receipt-discovery and session-history decision. Staff own visit closure. M-Pesa and card remain disabled pending provider integration.

Integrated source reconciliation used only the Customer source delta from 5e73a928c5d6e3089a5b4c53b374678159c511d4. The authenticated HTTPS fixture tests the published Customer wire routes. The JavaFX smoke test uses isolated test data for three locales. Neither test attests to a real cash collection. For the combined deployment acceptance, submit an order, open cash payment on Customer, confirm on Manager after collection, check the authoritative receipt on Customer, and repeat with a dropped Customer connection.
